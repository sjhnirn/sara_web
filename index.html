Need more details?

Here are a few answers to things clients usually ask before starting a project together with me.

What is your typical turnaround time for deliverables? +

For portrait and culinary sessions, a preview proofing gallery of highlights is ready within 48 hours. The complete suite of master-retouched images is delivered within 7 to 10 business days. Commercial and billboard campaigns follow custom expedited schedules upon production agreement.

Do you shoot on-location or in-studio in Tehran? +

Both. We maintain a fully equipped private studio in Tehran for controlled lighting, product packshots, and portraits. We also bring mobile high-end studio lighting on-location throughout Iran for architectural, industrial, culinary, and brand campaigns.

How are commercial licensing and billboard rights handled? +

All commercial commissions include full digital and print usage rights for web, social media, and marketing collateral. Comprehensive billboard, out-of-home (OOH), and nationwide advertising licenses are available and clearly defined in your agreement.

How do we prepare for creative direction and styling? +

Every booking includes a pre-production consultation. We will develop a collaborative moodboard, discuss lighting concepts, recommend wardrobe or props, and align on exact deliverable formats to ensure a seamless shoot day.
